📋 Adult vs. Infant Key Differences (Quick Review)

Step / Protocol 🧑 ADULT CPR / RB 👶 INFANT CPR / RB
Step 4: Checking Responsiveness "Hey hey hey are you okey? (2x)" — Tap the shoulder "Baby, Baby are you okey? (2x)" — Tap the feet
Step 6: Pulse Location (LLF) Carotid pulse (neck) — 1001 to 1010 Brachial pulse (inner upper arm) — 1001 to 1010
